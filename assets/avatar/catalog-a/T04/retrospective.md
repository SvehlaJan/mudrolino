# T04 — navy zip hoodie with orange lining retrospective

Status: fit-verified authored candidate. Final palette-A style/budget review, other head/hair compatibility and actual catalog registration remain open.

Reference: T04 in `assets/avatar/concepts/2026-10-04/catalog-a/01-tops.png`: navy long-sleeve zip hoodie, orange hood lining, ivory drawcords/zipper and angled front pocket openings. Master: `T04.blend`; independent exports: `T04.glb`, `body-T04.glb`. The shell, hanging lined hood and details have 9,566 vertices / 9,444 polygons, 24 groups and at most four normalized influences. The shell uses the measured long-sleeve template; conservative coverage includes overlapping elbow regions and preserves neck, wrists and hands.

The zipper ribbon is ray-projected onto the finished cloth rather than an approximate ellipse. Pocket openings and drawcords follow projected front points; the hood reuses the verified upper-torso/local-neck attachment from T02. Initial body/head and garment-layer checks were clear throughout both clips, but these checks alone could not prove that attached details stayed on the fabric.

A new detail-distance gate measured all ivory hardware/cord vertices and pocket seams against the cloth at rest and 163 quarter-frame walk/run poses. Nearest-vertex weight transfer produced a visible zipper kink and pocket-seam drift up to 11.42 mm; the combined ivory details reached 12.65 mm. Surface-triangle barycentric weight interpolation reduced pocket drift to 5.39 mm and smoothed the zipper, but one point still sat 12.06 mm from the collar even at rest. The reported authored position (x≈0, z≈1.417 m) identified the top of an oversized zipper pull, rather than a motion defect. Reducing/lowering that pull cleared the unchanged 12 mm gate. Current maximum detail-to-cloth distance is 8.03 mm over all 164 poses; the cords and rounded hardware intentionally have thickness and surface offsets.

The final independent master and T04/B01/F01/A01 combination each pass 163 quarter-frame walk/run poses with zero body/head and layer contacts. Six independent head gestures clear the hood and six cap attachment gestures clear the outfit. Rest plus 13 animated GLB comparisons agree within 6.07 micrometres. Fifteen current GLBs validate with zero errors/warnings/infos/hints; material checks pass. Desktop rest/walk/run and phone run render without page/console errors or failed GETs. Current source/export/browser/attachment/detail hashes are in `../outfits/T04-B01-F01-A01/verification-summary.json`. Expected availability HEAD cleanup is recorded.
